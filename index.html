Notes on emergent properties

Neil Yuanting Li

These notes explain how interactions among many constituents give rise to collective properties, from the pressure of a gas to the organization of living matter.

We begin with fluids and magnets, then extend the discussion to biology, neural activity, societies, computation, and the cosmos. The emphasis is on mechanisms: which variables matter, how they evolve, and what supports the explanation.

Introduction: what we mean by emergence →

The assumed background is graduate mathematics, basic graduate quantum mechanics, and introductory condensed matter. Other specialized terms are defined as they arise. Choose a shorter reading path or use the interactive models.
