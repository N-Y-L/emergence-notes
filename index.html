A bounded atlas of emergent phenomena

Emergence
One hundred ways a world appears

Neil Yuanting Li

One molecule does not have a viscosity. One bird does not make a flock. I want to understand what appears when the parts begin to matter to one another.

I follow a hundred examples through matter, living systems, minds, societies, computation, and the cosmos. Each begins with a puzzle, identifies the interactions that make the collective behavior possible, and asks where the explanation stops working.

Start here: what I mean by emergence →

Graduate physics and mathematics are welcome. Specialist knowledge of biology, economics, neuroscience, or computing is not assumed. Choose a shorter reading path or try six models.
